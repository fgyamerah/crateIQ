[x] Durable Audio Quality finding for FFmpeg-recovered malformed BPM audio.
    Done 2026-08-09: quality_findings_service.py owns a durable
    quality_review_findings table (upsert on track_id/reason_code/source)
    that quality_review_service merges into get_review()/refresh_preview()/
    update_decision() alongside its ffprobe snapshot, so the finding
    survives every ffprobe refresh. analysis_jobs_service records
    "recoverable_audio_decode_warning" (non-blocking) only when direct
    aubio showed real decode-error evidence and FFmpeg recovery succeeded,
    and "audio_decode_failed" (high-severity) only when both direct aubio
    and FFmpeg genuinely failed to decode. Surfaces through Needs Review's
    existing QUALITY aggregation. See CHANGELOG 2026-08-09.
